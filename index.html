step 2:

Theorize the best possible outcome of this task.

This may seem like a step in the wrong direction, that is, until you see...

FLIP-FLOP-AROO!!!

Take this perfect scenario and turn it on its head!

What you now have is your perfectly imperfect outcome.

step 4:

Accomplish!

Begin your task, keeping your end goal in mind.
